# Repeat Prescriptions

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## What is a repeat prescription?
A repeat prescription is a medicine your GP has agreed you can order again without an appointment. Only medicines on your repeat list can be ordered this way.

## How to order
- NHS App: the quickest way. Go to Prescriptions and choose your medicines.
- Online Requests form on our website.
- Paper slip: tick the items on the right-hand side of your last prescription and post it in the box at reception.
- We do NOT take repeat prescription requests over the phone, to avoid mistakes.

## How long it takes
Please allow 2 full working days (48 hours, not counting weekends or bank holidays) before collecting from your pharmacy. Order at least 1 week before you run out.

## Collecting
Prescriptions are sent electronically to your chosen (nominated) pharmacy. You can change your nominated pharmacy in the NHS App or by asking reception.

## Medication reviews
Most repeat medicines need a review once a year. If your review is due, we may issue a short supply and ask you to book a review.

## Medicines not on your repeat list
If you need a medicine that is not on your repeat list, you will need to speak to a clinician. Please book an appointment.
