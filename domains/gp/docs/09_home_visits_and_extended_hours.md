# Home Visits and Extended Hours

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## Home visits
Home visits are for patients who are housebound or too unwell to travel to the surgery.
- Please call before 10:30am to request a home visit.
- A GP will call you back first to decide if a visit is needed.
- Not having transport is not usually a reason for a home visit.

## Extended hours (local hub)
Evening and weekend appointments are offered at the local GP hub, shared with nearby practices.
- Weekday evenings: 6:30pm to 8:00pm
- Saturdays: 9:00am to 5:00pm
- These are booked through our reception team. You cannot book directly with the hub.
- Appointments may be with a GP, nurse or healthcare assistant from another local practice.
