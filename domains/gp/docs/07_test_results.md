# Test Results

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## How long results take
- Blood tests: usually 3 to 5 working days.
- Urine tests: usually 3 to 5 working days.
- X-rays and scans: usually 1 to 2 weeks.
- Some specialist tests can take longer.

## How to get your results
- NHS App: many results appear in your record once a GP has reviewed them.
- Phone: call 0116 496 0123 and press 3, between 11:00am and 6:00pm.
- For confidentiality, we can only give results to the patient, unless you have given us permission to share them with someone else.

## If a result needs action
If a GP needs to talk to you about a result, we will contact you by phone or text. Please make sure we have your current phone number.

## Explaining results
Reception staff can tell you whether a GP has reviewed a result and if any action is needed. They cannot explain what a result means medically. If you want a result explained, please book an appointment with a clinician.
