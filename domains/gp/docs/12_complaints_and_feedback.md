# Complaints and Feedback

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## Giving feedback
We welcome compliments and suggestions. You can use the feedback box in reception, the Online Requests form, or the NHS website review page.

## Making a complaint
- Speak to the Practice Manager first if you can. Many problems can be sorted out quickly.
- Or write to the Practice Manager at the surgery address or by email.
- We will acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days and aim to give a full reply within 20 working days.

## If you are not happy with our response
You can contact the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman (PHSO), which looks at NHS complaints independently.

## Behaviour policy
We will not tolerate abuse, threats or violence towards staff or other patients. Patients who are abusive may be removed from the practice list.
