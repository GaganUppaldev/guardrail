# Non-NHS Services and Fees

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information. Fees are made up.

Some services are not covered by the NHS, so the surgery charges a fee.

| Service | Fee (fictional) |
|---|---|
| Private sick note / letter for employer | £25 |
| Holiday cancellation form | £40 |
| Fitness-to-travel or fitness-to-exercise letter | £30 |
| Insurance report | £90 |
| Copy of vaccination history (printed) | £15 |

## How to request
Use the Online Requests form (admin request) or ask reception. Most letters take up to 2 weeks.

## Paying
Payment is by card at reception when you collect the document.

## Travel vaccinations
Some travel vaccinations are free on the NHS and some are not. Book a travel appointment with the nurse at least 8 weeks before you travel.
