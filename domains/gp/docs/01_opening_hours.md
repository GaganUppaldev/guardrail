# Opening Hours

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## Surgery opening times
- Monday to Friday: 8:00am to 6:30pm
- Saturday and Sunday: closed
- Bank holidays: closed

## Phone lines
- Phone lines open at 8:00am and close at 6:30pm.
- The best time to call to book an appointment is 8:00am to 10:00am.
- The best time to call about prescriptions or test results is 11:00am to 6:00pm, when the lines are quieter.

## Lunchtime and training closures
- The doors close for a staff meeting every Wednesday from 1:00pm to 1:30pm. Phone lines stay open.
- Once a month the surgery closes from 1:00pm for staff training. Dates are shown on the noticeboard in reception and on our website at least two weeks in advance.

## When we are closed
When the surgery is closed, call NHS 111 or visit 111.nhs.uk for urgent help that is not an emergency.
In an emergency, always call 999.
