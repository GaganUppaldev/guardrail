# Urgent Help and Out of Hours

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## Emergencies - call 999
Call 999 straight away for a life-threatening emergency, for example chest pain, signs of a stroke, severe difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding that will not stop, or if someone is unconscious.

## Urgent but not an emergency - NHS 111
If you need help urgently and the surgery is closed, or you are not sure where to go, call NHS 111 or go to 111.nhs.uk. It is free and open 24 hours a day.

## Mental health crisis
If you or someone else is in crisis, call NHS 111 and choose the mental health option, or call 999 if there is immediate danger. You can also call Samaritans free on 116 123, any time.

## Pharmacies
Local pharmacists can help with many minor illnesses without an appointment, through the NHS Pharmacy First service.
