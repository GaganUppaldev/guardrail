# Fit Notes (Sick Notes)

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## Do I need a fit note?
- For the first 7 days off sick (including weekends) you do NOT need a fit note from the GP. You can self-certify. Ask your employer for a self-certification form, or use form SC2.
- After 7 days, you need a fit note from a GP or another healthcare professional.

## How to request a fit note
- Use the Online Requests form and choose "Fit note".
- Or call 0116 496 0123 and press 4.
- A clinician may need to speak to you before issuing a fit note.

## Extending a fit note
If your fit note is about to run out and you are still unwell, request a new one at least 2 working days before it ends.

## How you receive it
Fit notes are usually sent by text message link or through the NHS App. Paper copies can be collected from reception.

## Fees
Fit notes are free. Some private sick notes or letters for employers, insurers or travel companies are not NHS services and a fee may apply (see Non-NHS Services and Fees).
