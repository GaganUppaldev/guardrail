# Registering as a Patient

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## Who can register
Anyone living in our practice area can register. You do not need proof of address, ID, an NHS number or immigration status to register with a GP.

## How to register
- Online: use the "Register with a GP surgery" service on the NHS website.
- In person: collect a registration form (GMS1) from reception, fill it in and hand it back.

## What happens next
- We aim to complete registration within 2 working days.
- You will get a text or letter when you are registered.
- New patients are invited to a new patient health check with a nurse.

## Registering children
Children can be registered by a parent or guardian using the same form. Please bring the child's red book if you have one.

## Practice area
Our practice area covers the fictional LE9 9 postcode area. Ask reception if you are unsure whether your address is inside the area.

## Temporary residents
If you are staying in the area for less than 3 months, you can register as a temporary resident.
