# Changing Your Details and Accessing Your Records

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## Changing your address, name or phone number
- Use the Online Requests form (admin request) or tell reception.
- Changing your name may need a document such as a marriage certificate or deed poll.
- If you move out of our practice area, you may need to register with a new surgery.

## Seeing your medical record
- You can see much of your record in the NHS App.
- To request a full copy of your record, make a Subject Access Request in writing or using the Online Requests form. This is free.
- We will respond within 1 calendar month.

## Sharing information with family or carers
If you want us to speak to a family member or carer about you, you need to give us permission. Ask reception for a consent form.

## Privacy
We follow UK GDPR. Our privacy notice explains how we use your information and is available on our website and at reception.
