# Booking Appointments

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## How to book
- By phone: call 0116 496 0123 and press 1. Lines are busiest from 8:00am to 10:00am.
- Online: through the NHS App or the Online Requests form on our website.
- In person: at reception during opening hours.

## Types of appointment
- Same-day appointments: released at 8:00am each weekday for problems that need to be seen today.
- Routine appointments: can be booked up to 4 weeks ahead.
- Telephone appointments: a clinician calls you back. You will be given a time window.
- Nurse appointments: for blood tests, dressings, smear tests, vaccinations and long-term condition reviews.
- Extended hours: evening and Saturday appointments are available at the local hub. Ask reception.

## Cancelling or changing
Please cancel at least 24 hours before if you cannot attend, so someone else can use the slot.
You can cancel by phone, through the NHS App, or by replying CANCEL to your text reminder.

## Missed appointments
If you miss 3 appointments in 12 months without telling us, we will send you a letter and may ask to discuss this with you.

## Interpreters
If you need an interpreter, tell us when you book. It is free and we may book a longer appointment.
