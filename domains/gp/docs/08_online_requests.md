# Online Requests

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT - Riverside Lane Surgery is a fictional practice. Not real information.

## What is it?
The Online Requests form on our website lets you contact the surgery without phoning. It is open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 6:30pm.

## Two types of request
- Admin requests: for example repeat prescription queries, fit notes, letters, changing your details or asking about a referral.
- Medical requests: for non-urgent health problems. A clinician reads every medical request and decides on the right next step.

## How quickly we respond
- Admin requests: within 2 working days.
- Medical requests: we aim to contact you by the end of the next working day.

## Do NOT use online requests for emergencies
Online requests are not checked straight away. If you think you need urgent help, call NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.

## Help using the form
If you cannot use the form, call reception and a member of staff can fill it in with you over the phone.
